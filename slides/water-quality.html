Water quality

Water quality, built into the plan

The site drains to Cowpen Slough. Following the region's Water Quality Playbook, the plan reduces nutrients at the source, catches what runs off, and teaches the practices that sustain it.

Reduce

Keep nutrients on the land

  • Stock only what the pasture carries, and rotate the herd so wet ground can rest.
  • Collect and compost manure and bedding from the pens and barns.
Remove

Catch and treat what runs off

  • A retention area and bioswale treat runoff from the parking, drives, and roofs.
  • Washdown water is collected, never discharged to pasture, wetlands, or ditches.
  • Wooded and wet edges stay planted between the pastures and the slough.
Sustain

Teach it, and show it working

  • Learning by doing: beginning ranchers and 4-H youth learn water quality best management practices, including grazing, manure, and soil management.
  • A working demonstration of stormwater features and water quality practices, open to the public.
1 in 3

Only about a third of Sarasota County's farms under 10 acres follow agricultural best management practices, and small animal farms and hobbyists are the hardest group to reach. They are exactly who this center teaches.
