Over to you

Where we'd value your input

Topics for the tables. Tell us where the plan is wrong, weak, or missing something.

Pasture and rotation

  • Do the paddock sizes and gates support a workable rotation?
  • Are the stocking rates right for this forage?
  • What animal weights should set the head count?

Water and wet ground

  • Where should troughs and water lines go?
  • How should grazing be timed on the wettest soils?
  • Should cattle be fenced out of the slough, ponds, and wet edges, and how wide should that buffer be?
  • How sacred is the historical slough bed that crosses the site? It conflicts with some of the program and complicates the civil design.
  • Which floodplain compensation option, and is more freeboard for the livestock buildings worth the cost?

Wooded pasture

  • How should the oak hammock paddocks be grazed?
  • Is there a case for improving forage under the pines and palms?
  • Which trees matter most to protect?

Animals and operations

  • What herd size makes the Beginning Rancher program work as a teaching operation?
  • How should manure and washdown water be handled?
  • What have we left out of the barns and handling areas?
