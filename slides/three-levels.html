Why we're here

One plan, three Levels

The study is organized in three Levels of investment. Every part of the plan is shown at each one. The site work is not phased: permitting, stormwater, and floodplain compensation are all done at Level 1, and the Levels phase what is built inside it.

Level 1

Lightest touch

The least it takes to open and operate. Every existing building is reused with minimum renovation.

Open the doors
Level 2

Better management

The same land with more program capacity, and the first new buildings as programs fill up.

Grow with demand
Level 3

Best case

The full program, with the site pasture managed at its best.

The ceiling we plan to
We designed Level 3 first, then worked back to Level 1, so each Level builds on the one before it.
The approach

The land sets the herd, the programs set the buildings, and the Levels let it grow.
