Working cattle: the Bud Box

    Cattle want to go back the way they came, and to move around the person working them. The box uses both instincts. Cattle flow through it; they are never held in it.

    Option B uses this yard mirrored (the loop on its west side) and sized for four cattle, 36' × 42' with 12' corner panels. Diagram to scale, in feet. Layout from the study plan, after the Priefert Bud Box. No trailer enclosure: trailers unload from the loop straight into the holding pen. Sizes follow extension guidance (K-State MF3349, Missouri G2003): box 12' wide for handlers on foot and 20' deep. The 16' single-file alley plus the chute holds four cattle.
