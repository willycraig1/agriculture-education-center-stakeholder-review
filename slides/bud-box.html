Working cattle: the Bud Box

    Cattle want to go back the way they came, and to move around the person working them. The box uses both instincts. Cattle flow through it; they are never held in it.

    Diagram to scale, in feet. Layout from the study plan, after the Priefert Bud Box, with a gated trailer stop in the parking loop. Sizes follow extension guidance (K-State MF3349, Missouri G2003): box 12' wide for handlers on foot and 20' deep; 24' straight single-file alley.
