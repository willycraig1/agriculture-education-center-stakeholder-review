The plan

One barn for swine, sheep, and goats

One kind of pen for all three. The hogs move into the existing barn at Level 1, swap into a new barn at Level 2, and that barn grows south at Level 3.

The 6' × 10' pen

pens to a wing: two rows of 6' × 10' pens facing an 8' aisle, in a 36'-wide barn.

Any pen can take a hog, a sheep, or a goat, so the mix can follow enrollment from year to year.

One barn, two steps

Barn A is two wings with a roofed dogtrot between them, opening to its turnout on the west. Barn B extends it south four 12' bays.
