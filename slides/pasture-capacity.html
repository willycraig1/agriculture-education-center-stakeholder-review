Act 3 · What the land can carry

Pasture-based cattle capacity

Mature-cow equivalents (CE) the fenced pasture can carry at each investment Level. Level 3 is the best case. Level 1 is the minimum needed to operate.

CE is mature-cow-equivalent grazing demand. The pasture herd belongs to the Beginning Rancher program. 4-H cow & calf projects are housed in 12'×24' pens and sized separately. Head count may be higher where cattle are lighter, but the final number should be set from expected animal weights and forage demand.

Stocking rates: UF/IFAS AN155 planning values (conservative 4 ac/CE open, 25 ac/CE wooded; improved rotational 2 and 10), to be refined with UF/IFAS for forage condition and animal type. Acreages: fencing plan A0.1 and the pasture worksheet.
