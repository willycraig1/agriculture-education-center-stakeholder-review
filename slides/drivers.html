What guides the plan

From the Owner's goals to design drivers

Four goals do most of the work in shaping this plan.

Pasture is scarce

Pasture is what this community is short on, so the plan sizes the herd to the land the site has.

Drives: herd size, fencing

Reuse first

The Red House and the existing barns go back to work before anything new is built, keeping the site's Old Florida character and limiting costs.

Drives: what we keep

Grow by module

Pens and barns are added in modules as programs fill up. Nothing is locked to a fixed animal count.

Drives: how it grows

Protect the water

Water quality is a priority from the start, built into the design and into daily operations: grazing, washdown, manure management, and more.

Drives: layout, timing
