Act 2 · What the program needs

Two cattle programs, more animals, and people

Each user of the farm needs something different from it.

Animals

Beginning Rancher herd

Lives on pasture. No pens. Needs fencing, water, and a corral with chute and scale for working the herd.

4-H cow & calf projects

Housed in 12' × 24' pens, one project per pen, with turnout, washdown, and feed and tack storage.

Swine, sheep, and goats

Shaded, well-ventilated small-animal pens on a 6' × 10' module, sturdy enough for hogs and easy to clean out. Any pen can take any of the three.

Poultry

Predator-safe poultry housing, apart from the other animals.

People

Teaching

A classroom and workshop for about 50, and a demonstration kitchen.

Meeting

A conference room for 20 to 25, near the lobby and restrooms.

Staff

Offices for 5 staff, with sight of the entry.

Growing

A small horticulture teaching area.

Showing

Space for demonstrations, turnout, and community events.

Ground rules

Animal care standardThe Ag Guide (4th ed.) sets the bar for space, water, shade, ventilation, and handling.
Modular and flexiblePens, panels, and gates that can be rearranged as demand changes.
Safe separationPublic, staff, animal-care, and service areas kept distinct, with supervised access for youth.
Water quality firstStormwater, washdown, and manure handled so nothing runs unmanaged to the slough or wetlands.
