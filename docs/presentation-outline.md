# Presentation Outline: Owner Stakeholder Review (Wed 2026-10-14)

Working draft. Target is 30–45 min: about 30 min of content plus Q&A, with optional slides that can be dropped.

## Storyline

**The land decides the herd, the herd decides the buildings.** We start with what the site can carry,
turn that into a number of cattle, size the cattle facilities from that number, and then fit the other
programs and the Red House around them. Every section uses the same three investment **Levels**
(Level 1 = minimum functional, up to Ultimate), so the Owner can see the cost and the benefit of
each step up.

## Section 5 tension to resolve before Wednesday

- The study plan (A0.2) shows **51 cattle pens** (7 + 9 + 9 + 9 + 17) plus 32 hog pens.
- Pasture supports roughly **8–12 project cattle at launch** and **30–32 at full build-out** (infographic estimate).
- The Ag Guide (Table 6-1, p. 92) needs about 20–50 sf per head under cover, depending on class and bedding.
  A 12'×24' pen is 288 sf, so the module is generous and pen count should follow the number of project
  animals and whether pens are individual or shared.
- **We need to know whether the pens hold the pasture herd, or pen-fed show/project animals on top of it.**
  The answer sets the barn size, so it goes first.
