# Drafting standards for the site plan sheets

The architect's rules, collected from the reviews of 2026-11-18. Check a revised sheet against this list before issuing it.

## Orientation

- North is to the left on every sheet. East is up, south is to the right, and west is down.

## Topography

- Contours are at 1' intervals only. Do not add intermediate lines.
- Contours are continuous. No line stops short or breaks at a path.
- Adjacent contours stay a few feet apart. The 21' line never touches the 22' line.
- Show the swales and ponds with contours, so they do not read as a flooded pasture.
- Water levels are drawn as water lines, not as contours.

## Ground plane

- Paths and the raised pad are the same color as the surrounding high ground. Only the contours and culverts show them.
- The pad reads from its 22' contour. Do not outline the pad edge.
- The Path A ramp reads from the 22' contour running out along the path.

## Swales and ponds

- Keep wide radii. Remove sharp corners where a path meets the top of bank.
- Avoid disjointed or kinked contours, and ease steep banks around corners.
- Show the swale as the retention pond's littoral shelf.

## Culverts

- Draw every culvert as one straight line.
- Extend each culvert to the invert of the swale on both sides.

## Trees and fences

- Surveyed trees are drawn above the grading, whether or not that is realistic.
- Pasture gates are drawn at half their original line weight.
- Do not label the pond's 10' fence offset on the sheets.

## Text

- Use the Oxford comma in every list of three or more.
