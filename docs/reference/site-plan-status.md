# Site plan status

As of 2026-11-18. Values are in `figures.md`.

## What the plan shows today

Sheets AS-1 (overall, 1" = 100') and AS-2 (campus, 1" = 40') carry the owner's stormwater direction at Level 3.

- The working barnyard sits on one raised pad at BFE + 1'.
- The retention pond opens into an expanded slough-bed swale, which works as the pond's littoral shelf.
- The north triangle has its own swale and small retention pond, which discharges under the road to the bend in the main swale.
- Paths A, B, and C stay on high ground and cross the swale centerlines on culverts.
