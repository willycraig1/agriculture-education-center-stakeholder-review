# County interior standards for the Red House

What Sarasota County's furniture and interior standards ask for, boiled down so the Red House plans can show it. The source PDFs stay in the project folder under `From Owner/County Standards/`:

| Source | Pages | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| Facilities Design Standard (received Dec 2025) | 6 | Office and workstation types with sizes and contents (Design Standards 13, 15, 22, 23, 24) |
| Sarasota County Interior Standards r1 | 12 | Workstation and office layouts, flooring, and finish colours |
| Furniture Specification, County Administration Center, R8 (3 May 2024) | 68 | Every furniture type with product lines and finishes, written for the Administration Center |

The furniture specification was written for one building, so its products and finishes are the County's preferred palette, not a rule for every site. Use the type codes below (OFC-1.1, TBL-5 and so on) on the plans so the County can match them to its standard.

## What the standards do not cover

- **Room lighting.** None of the three documents sets light levels, fixtures, or controls. The only lighting named is task lighting under the overhead cabinets in offices. Ask the County which lighting standard it uses (light levels, LED fixture type, occupancy sensors, and daylight controls) before the Red House lighting is drawn.
- Ceilings, wall paint, window treatments, acoustics, signage, and IT rooms. Not addressed.
- Kitchens and restrooms. Only the breakroom furniture is covered, not the kitchen equipment or casework.

## What this means for the plans

- **Office sizes.** Draw offices at 10' x 12' (manager) or 12' x 15' (director), measured inside the walls. The standard shows two layouts of each: windows on the back wall or on a side wall. The Red House offices are set by the existing walls, so note any office that is smaller and say which items fit.
- **Power and data.** Every desk is height-adjustable with desktop power (2 outlets, USB-A, and USB-C). Workstations need 4 duplex outlets each, touchdown desks 2. Conference tables 6' and longer get a power and data module, so plan a floor box under large tables. Training tables are daisy-chained, so the classroom needs floor or wall power along the table rows.
- **Storage.** Allow wall space for 36"-wide lateral files and bookcases in each office, and for overhead cabinets above the credenza.
- **Classroom.** Tables fold and nest on casters, so the event space needs a storage alcove for folded tables and stacked chairs.

The Red House is a historic farmhouse with a demo kitchen and animal-program traffic. Walk-off carpet at every entry and LVT in the classroom, kitchen, and breakroom fit both the standard and that use. Confirm with the County whether carpet is wanted in the offices or whether LVT throughout is acceptable.

## Open questions for the County

1. Which lighting standard applies (light levels, fixtures, and controls)?
2. Are the Administration Center products required, or only the types, sizes, and colours?
3. Do the offices need to meet the 10' x 12' size, or can the historic rooms be smaller?
4. Carpet or LVT in the offices?
