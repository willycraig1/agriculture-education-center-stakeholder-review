# Report reference, archived 2026-10-19

A frozen, self-contained copy of the full stakeholder deck (Option B, 18 slides) as it stood when the owner asked
for a shorter, vision-first presentation (Lee Hayes Byron, comments of 2026-10-19). This is the detailed version:
every design decision with its how and why. Keep it as the reference for the final report, which is the project's
deliverable.

- View it at `archive/report-reference-2026-10-19/` on the Pages site (open `index.html`), or open `index.html`
  from this folder; it runs without a server.
- Copied from the working tree, including the 16' cattle aisle and McIntosh barn-reference changes that were not
  yet committed at the time. The build tools and source drawings that produced it are in the repo's `tools/` and
  `source/` folders; the copies here are output only.
- The presentation going forward (internal audience: the owner and her staff) starts from the Level 3 vision and
  walks back to Level 1, with the bonus pasture and the Solid Waste property left out.
